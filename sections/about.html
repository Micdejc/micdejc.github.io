01 / ABOUT

Cybersecurity Thought Leader. Educator. Researcher

Cybersecurity thought leader, associate lecturer and researcher specialising in Cybersecurity, AI security, with a focus on adversarial AI and defensive mechanisms. Creator of educational cybersecurity content on LinkedIn through the Cyber Dramas series and Bash Family ventures.

I contribute to the wider cybersecurity and AI community through professional, academic and industry engagement. I am an Expert Volunteer Panel member of the UK Cyber Security Council, the UK's only chartered body authorised to award professional titles in cybersecurity, and a mentor at Startupbootcamp, ranked the #1 startup accelerator in Europe, supporting emerging companies working to turn innovative ideas into real-world solutions.

In 2025, I became the first Black African recipient from Cameroon of a Best Paper Award at UK Workshop on Computational Intelligence (UKCI), recognising my research on the security of large language models. I also serve as an Expert Analyst for Heads Talk Analysis Media, featured among the Top 10 Apple Podcasts Government charts, where I contribute perspectives on cybersecurity, AI, and emerging technologies.

As an Associate Lecturer at the University of the West of England (UWE Bristol), I teach and assess BSc and MSc students across cybersecurity, operating systems, computer networks, digital forensics, and emerging technologies, while supervising MSc research in cybersecurity and AI. My academic contributions also include peer review for Elsevier's Information Sciences, a leading Q1 journal, and Cambridge University Press (from the prestigious University of Cambridge in the UK), contributing to the evaluation and advancement of research in AI and information security.
