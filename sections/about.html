01 / ABOUT

Cybersecurity Thought Leader. Educator. Researcher

Cybersecurity thought leader and researcher specialising in AI security and Large Language Model security, focusing on jailbreak attacks, adversarial AI, safety evaluation and defensive mechanisms.

I contribute to the wider cybersecurity and AI community through professional, academic and industry engagement. I am an Expert Panel member of the UK Cyber Security Council, the UK's only chartered body authorised to award professional titles in cybersecurity, and a mentor at Startupbootcamp, ranked the #1 startup accelerator in Europe.

In 2025, I became the first Black African recipient of a Best Paper Award at UKCI, recognising my research on the security of large language models. I also serve as an Expert Analyst for Heads Talk Analysis Media, featured among the Top 10 Apple Podcasts Government charts, where I contribute perspectives on cybersecurity and emerging technology.

My academic contributions extend to peer review for Information Sciences, a leading Q1 journal published by Elsevier, reflecting my involvement in the evaluation and advancement of research in AI and information security.
